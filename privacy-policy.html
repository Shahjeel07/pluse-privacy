Privacy Policy for Pulse Rivals

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Pulse Rivals ("the app") is a mobile arcade game by jeel shah ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information we store

The app does not require an account. Your game progress (level, coins, XP, scores, settings) is stored only on your device in local storage. We do not receive or store it on any server. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. Where required, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later in Settings > Ad Privacy Choices. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. The only third party that receives data through the app is Google AdMob, as described above.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this page.
